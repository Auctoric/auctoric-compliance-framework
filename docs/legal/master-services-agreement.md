## 1. Definitions

* **"Affiliate"** means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with the subject entity. "Control" means direct or indirect ownership of more than 50% of the voting interests.
* **"AI Models"** means the machine learning architectures, foundation models, fine-tuned weights, embeddings, inference engines, and algorithms made available through the Services.
* **"Auctoric Platform"** or **"Services"** means the suite of cloud-hosted AI governance, workflow orchestration, inference endpoints, agentic coordination engines, and software-as-a-service applications provided by Auctoric as specified in an Order Form.
* **"Authorized User"** means an employee, contractor, agent, or designated system user authorized by Customer to access the Services under Customer’s account.
* **"Customer Content"** means all electronic data, text, prompts, queries, files, audio, video, vector stores, ground-truth labels, or materials submitted by or on behalf of Customer or Authorized Users to the Services ("**Inputs**"), and the resulting outputs, predictions, or synthetic data generated by the Services for Customer ("**Outputs**").
* **"Documentation"** means the technical specifications, user guides, API manuals, and security whitepapers published or made available by Auctoric at `[https://docs.auctoric.com]`, as updated from time to time.
* **"Order Form"** means an ordering document, statement of work, or online purchasing flow specifying the Services, fees, usage capacities, and subscription term agreed upon between Auctoric and Customer.
* **"Service Level Agreement (SLA)"** means the uptime, latency, and operational commitments set forth in the Auctoric SLA located in `[docs/security-trust/system-security-plan-summary.md]` or an applicable Order Form.

## 2. Platform Access and Use Rights

### 2.1 Provision of Services
Auctoric shall make the Services available to Customer pursuant to this Agreement, the Documentation, and applicable Order Forms during the Subscription Term. Auctoric grants Customer a worldwide, non-exclusive, non-transferable (except as permitted under Section 13.3) right to access and use the Auctoric Platform solely for Customer’s internal business operations.

### 2.2 Authorized Users and Credentials
Customer is responsible for all activity occurring under its Authorized User accounts. Customer shall maintain the confidentiality of all API tokens, client secrets, and authentication credentials, and promptly notify Auctoric at `[security@auctoric.com]` upon becoming aware of any unauthorized credential access or compromise.

### 2.3 Usage Limits and API Quotas
Services are subject to usage limits defined in the applicable Order Form (e.g., token consumption, inference transactions per second (TPS), concurrent agentic threads, storage volume). If Customer exceeds a contractual usage limit, Auctoric will work with Customer to scale capacity and bill for excess usage at the predetermined rate specified in the Order Form.

## 3. Customer Responsibilities and Prohibited Uses

### 3.1 Compliance with Acceptable Use Policy
Customer agrees to adhere to the Auctoric Acceptable Use Policy (`[docs/legal/acceptable-use-policy.md]`). Customer shall not:
1. Reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or derive source code or underlying neural model architectures of the Services;
2. Use the Services or Outputs to train, distill, or evaluate competing machine learning models or services without explicit written permission from Auctoric;
3. Bypass, probe, or evade rate limits, authentication barriers, guardrails, or safety classifiers;
4. Transmit any malicious code, prompt injection attacks, adversarial jailbreaks, or harmful automation scripts;
5. Process unlawful, infringing, or defamatory materials or utilize the platform for autonomous physical harm, weapons design, or biometric categorization prohibited by the EU AI Act or applicable laws.

## 4. Intellectual Property Rights & AI Ownership

### 4.1 Auctoric Intellectual Property
Auctoric and its licensors retain all right, title, and interest (including all patent, copyright, trademark, and trade secret rights) in and to:
1. The Auctoric Platform, software, APIs, SDKs, agentic runtime engines, and Documentation;
2. Base foundational model weights, system prompts, guardrail heuristics, fine-tuning infrastructure, and synthetic validation benchmarks created by Auctoric;
3. Any de-identified, aggregated telemetry and operational performance metrics generated through platform operations.

### 4.2 Customer Content & Ownership of AI Outputs
As between Auctoric and Customer:
1. **Inputs:** Customer retains exclusive ownership of and all intellectual property rights in Customer Inputs.
2. **Outputs:** To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Auctoric assigns to Customer all right, title, and interest in and to Customer Outputs generated specifically for Customer from Customer Inputs.
3. **No Training on Customer Data:** Auctoric explicitly agrees and warrants that **Customer Inputs and Outputs will NOT be used to train, retrain, align, or improve Auctoric’s base public models or third-party foundation models**, unless Customer expressly opts in via a signed Model Improvement Addendum. See `[docs/ai-governance/ai-dpa-model-non-retention-addendum.md]`.

### 4.3 Feedback
If Customer provides feedback, suggestions, or feature requests to Auctoric, Auctoric may freely use and incorporate such feedback into its platform without restriction or obligation to Customer.

## 10. Term, Renewal, and Termination

### 10.1 Term of Agreement
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms have expired or been terminated.

### 10.2 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate this Agreement:
1. Upon thirty (30) days’ written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within the 30-day period;
2. Immediately if the other party becomes insolvent, enters receivership, or makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors.

### 10.3 Post-Termination Data Portability and Deletion
Upon request within thirty (30) days after termination, Auctoric will make Customer Content available for export. After such period, Auctoric will purge and overwrite all Customer Content from active databases in accordance with NIST SP 800-88 Rev 1 media sanitization standards.
